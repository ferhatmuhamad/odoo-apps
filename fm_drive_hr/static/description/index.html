Team Drive for Employees

Per-employee document archives, on top of Team Drive

This is a free add-on, not a standalone app. It needs Team Drive and Odoo's Employees app; without Team Drive, Odoo will refuse to install it. Nothing extra to pay — if you own Team Drive, this is already yours, and it installs itself as soon as both are present.

HR paperwork has a shape of its own: every employee has the same handful of folders, most documents expire, and who may read them is not the usual answer. This add-on teaches Team Drive that shape — and it is kept separate precisely so that a company with no HR app is never forced to install one just to get a file drive.

What it adds

An archive per employee

Every employee gets a folder, built from templates you edit from a menu rather than from code: Contracts, Diplomas, Certificates, whatever your company keeps. New employees get theirs on creation; a nightly job backfills anyone added through a bulk import.

Who may look

HR reads every archive, HR Managers maintain them, an employee sees only their own — and a direct manager gets nothing by default. Warning letters and contracts are not a line manager's business. When there is a real need, HR shares the document explicitly, and that sharing lands in the audit log.

Documents that expire on their own

A folder template can carry a lifetime, so a file dropped into “Contracts” is dated twelve months out without anyone typing a date. The reminder then reaches the file owner, the employee concerned, and HR Management — the people who can actually renew it.

Share with a whole department

Adds “one department” next to “specific people” in the Share dialog. Child departments inherit it, so sharing with Manufacturing reaches the teams underneath without naming them one by one.

Available for Odoo 17.0, 18.0 and 19.0, on both Community and Enterprise. Uninstalling it never deletes an archive: the folders fall back to the ordinary personal kind and keep every file inside them.
